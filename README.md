# Which Careers Offer the Best Combination of Pay and Growth?

**Hamza Abulaila** — INFO 4617 Web Data Science, University of Colorado Boulder

Most people pick a career with salary in mind, but pay on its own doesn't say much about whether
a job will actually be there when you graduate. This project looks at 12 occupations that college
students realistically consider and asks how median annual wages line up with projected employment
growth and annual job openings in the United States from 2025 to 2035. The data comes from two
public U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics sources, the Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics
tables and the Employment Projections database, joined on occupation codes. I expect the
best-paying jobs won't automatically be the ones with the most openings, and the point is to see
which careers do well on more than one measure instead of just one.
